    Drydock beta: notes & credits

    An unofficial, fan-made ship viewer and editor for Cosmoteer: Starship Architect & Commander. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Walternate Realities.

    Your files

    Everything runs in your browser. Ship files and images you open are never uploaded anywhere. Drydock never changes your original file; saving always makes a new copy, so keep a backup before replacing ships in your Saved Ships folder.

    Known limits

    • The thermal canister missile factory’s price (18,000) is worked out from its materials; other factories cost a few percent more in game, so it may be slightly low.
    • Crew needed counts each module’s required crew; the game’s higher “recommended” figure (extra hands for ammo and power) isn’t included. Roles and crew priorities on loaded ships are kept, but can’t be edited here.
    • Build-area edges and the 8 flight directions follow how saved ships behave; tell us if the game disagrees.
    • Very large hulls (hundreds of thousands of parts) can be slow to edit and save.
